BeeTreeCraft stands on other people's work. This page credits it and points you to the originals.

## Behavior tree reading

- [*Behavior Trees in Robotics and AI: An Introduction*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.00084), Michele Colledanchise and Petter Ögren (CRC Press, 2018). The node-type definitions and the state-machine comparison on the [Learn](/learn/) pages follow its Chapter 1. [Free PDF on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1709.00084v1).
- [Behavior tree](https://grokipedia.com/page/Behavior_tree) on Grokipedia. Inspiration for the scope of [Behavior trees and their history](/learn/behavior-trees/).

## Game AI Pro chapters

[Game AI Pro](http://www.gameaipro.com/) is a series of practitioner-written game AI books, with every chapter free online. These chapters cover behavior trees:

- [Behavior Selection Algorithms: An Overview](http://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro/GameAIPro_Chapter04_Behavior_Selection_Algorithms.pdf) (Game AI Pro, chapter 4)
- [The Behavior Tree Starter Kit](http://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro/GameAIPro_Chapter06_The_Behavior_Tree_Starter_Kit.pdf) (Game AI Pro, chapter 6)
- [Real-World Behavior Trees in Script](http://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro/GameAIPro_Chapter07_Real-World_Behavior_Trees_in_Script.pdf) (Game AI Pro, chapter 7)
- [Simulating Behavior Trees: A Behavior Tree / Planner Hybrid Approach](http://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro/GameAIPro_Chapter08_Simulating_Behavior_Trees.pdf) (Game AI Pro, chapter 8)
- [Building Utility Decisions into Your Existing Behavior Tree](http://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro/GameAIPro_Chapter10_Building_Utility_Decisions_into_Your_Existing_Behavior_Tree.pdf) (Game AI Pro, chapter 10)
- [Building a Risk-Free Environment to Enhance Prototyping: Hinted-Execution Behavior Trees](http://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro2/GameAIPro2_Chapter10_Building_a_Risk-Free_Environment_to_Enhance_Prototyping.pdf) (Game AI Pro 2, chapter 10)
- [Overcoming Pitfalls in Behavior Tree Design](http://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro3/GameAIPro3_Chapter09_Overcoming_Pitfalls_in_Behavior_Tree_Design.pdf) (Game AI Pro 3, chapter 9)
- [Template Tricks for Data-Driven Behavior Trees](http://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIProOnlineEdition2021/GameAIProOnlineEdition2021_Chapter13_Template_Tricks_for_Data-Driven_Behavior_Trees.pdf) (Game AI Pro Online Edition 2021, chapter 13)
