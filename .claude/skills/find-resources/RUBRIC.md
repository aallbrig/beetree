# Resource rubric

Score each criterion 0, 1 or 2. Include a resource at **9 or more of 14**, and only when Accuracy, Access and Safety are not 0.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Authority** | anonymous or unverifiable | a practitioner with some track record | recognised author or publisher in game AI or robotics (shipped games, peer review, a known press or conference) |
| **Accuracy and depth** | wrong or shallow | mostly right, thin | correct, specific, shows working examples or evidence |
| **Relevance** | tangential to behavior trees | covers them as one topic among many | centred on behavior trees, or on a technique this site teaches (see `VISION.md`) |
| **Longevity** | outdated, or tied to a dead tool | still useful, somewhat dated | timeless concepts, or maintained |
| **Access** | paywalled, login wall, unstable URL | free with friction (ads, popups, a sign-up) | free, open and a stable URL |
| **Originality** | a rehash of better sources | adds some perspective | adds a new idea, example or case study |
| **Safety** | suspicious scripts, downloads or tracking | plain ads | a reputable host, https, nothing to install |

A source scoring below 9 goes in `REJECTED.md` with its score and a reason, so it is not evaluated again.
